Privacy Policy

App: My Finance (iOS)
Last updated: October 29, 2026

What the app does

My Finance is a personal finance app. It stores your financial entries (such as expenses) on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, it also syncs those entries to a Google Sheet in your own Google account.

Information we access

  • Your Google account email, used only to show which account is signed in.
  • Your Google Sheets data, accessed only to read and write the spreadsheet used for syncing your expenses.

How information is used

Google data is used only to provide the sync feature inside the app. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Your data stays on your device and in your own Google Sheet. The developer does not run any server that receives your data. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party. Sign-in tokens are stored securely on your device.

Google API Services User Data Policy

My Finance's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can sign out of the app at any time, and you can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. You can delete the Google Sheet from your own Google Drive at any time.
